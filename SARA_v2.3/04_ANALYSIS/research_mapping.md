# Research Mapping

## Minimum Schema

- Title
- Author(s)
- Year
- Research type
- Research problem
- Purpose
- Research question(s)
- Hypothesis(es), if applicable
- Theoretical framework
- Data / participants
- Research design
- Analysis method
- Main findings
- Limitations
- Contribution

## Variable Policy

IV / DV / mediator / moderator / effect size 등은 quantitative 또는 해당 구조가 존재하는 연구에서만 기록한다.
